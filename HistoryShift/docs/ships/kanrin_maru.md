## Sources

- Built by L. Smit en Zoon, Kinderdijk; ~50 m long; Japan's first screw-propelled steam corvette; 100 hp coal-fired engine — <https://wikiwand.com/en/articles/Japanese_warship_Kanrin_Maru>
- Ordered by the shogunate from the Netherlands in 1853; delivered 21 Sep 1857 as 'Japan' — <https://wikiwand.com/en/articles/Japanese_warship_Kanrin_Maru>
- Under Katsu Kaishu as captain, escort to the 1860 embassy: left Uraga 10 Feb 1860, reached San Francisco 17 Mar, returned to Uraga 23 Jun — <https://www.mofa.go.jp/mofaj/annai/honsho/shiryo/akebono/13.html>
- Sailed under canvas at sea; the engine was used only against headwinds and entering or leaving port — <https://intojapanwaraku.com/rock/culture-rock/168013/>
- 11 Americans aboard including Lt John M. Brooke — <https://intojapanwaraku.com/rock/culture-rock/168013/>
- Fukuzawa Yukichi sailed as servant to Kimura Settsu-no-kami (admiral) — <https://objecthub.keio.ac.jp/en/object/15255>
- Wrecked off Saraki Point (Kikonai) 1871 carrying ~401 former Sendai-domain samurai and families; wreck not found — <https://tabi-mag.jp/ho1272/>
